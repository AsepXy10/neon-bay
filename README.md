Neon Bay is an original open-world crime game built for the make.fun "Make GTA 6" bounty. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs from 0 to 5 stars — at three stars and above the NBPD throws roadblocks across the road ahead of you, so ram through them or go around — and work through six story missions from the fixer Vex. Traffic and pedestrians fill the streets, the city runs a day/night cycle, and the HUD has a live minimap — all art, story, and sound made for this game, nothing taken from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD, aim with the mouse, click to shoot, E to enter or exit a car, F to punch, R to reload, M to mute, N to skip time.
